# Model card

Random Forest baseline trained on 14 handcrafted waveform statistics. The model predicts three artificial waveform generator settings. Training uses subject-disjoint partitions and a held-out noisy test set. Reported scores must come from executing the repository, not from clinical studies.

**Intended use:** ML teaching, synthetic data benchmarking, reproducibility, robustness experiments.

**Not intended:** diagnosis, screening, monitoring, triage, treatment, or deployment with patient ECG data.
